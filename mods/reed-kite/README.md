# Reed Kite

Hold the kite while you fall. Your fall slows, and looking left or right drifts you that way. Sneak to fold it and drop normally. It does nothing on the ground, in water, while you are flying with an elytra, or while you are riding.

A controlled glide does not build fall damage. If you let go high up, the rest of the fall is yours. Gliding spends durability and a little hunger, so it is a descent, not a flight across the map. It does not boost.

## Recipe

Paper, a stick, and string. Repair it with paper. It also appears in the Tools and Utilities creative tab.

## Config

`config/reed-kite.json`

- `maxFallSpeed` (default 0.13) the fastest you fall while the kite is open
- `drift` (default 0.035) how hard looking steers you
- `maxHorizontal` (default 0.45) top sideways speed
- `exhaustionPerSecond` (default 0.12)
- `durabilityPerSecond` (default 1)

## Environment

Install it on both sides. The server sets your fall while the kite is in either hand.
